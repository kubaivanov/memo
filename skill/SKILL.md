Start with `memo wake`. Follow any requests to page or compress; keep lasting decisions and open blockers. After a confirmed outcome, write one short, factual `memo note` without secrets. Use `memo recall` or `memo show` for details. Correct mistakes with `memo amend` or `memo retract`, never by editing the history.
